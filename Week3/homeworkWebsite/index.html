Tall Dark and Handsome

Enchantment... When entering the battlefield, the target creature professes their love to this handsome cat, unable to attack while this card is in play.

  • After 3 turns, the target creature goes to your hand
  • During your turn, this card can be directed towards any creature
  • Inflicts love at first sight
He holds out his paw, you gaze into his dreamy eyes. He purrs-
"I've been looking for you all my nine lives."
